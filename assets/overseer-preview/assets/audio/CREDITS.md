All three pages are published by the `freesound_community` account. These are
used as generic effects for an original, fictional orange car. The original
recording names are retained for attribution, not manufacturer endorsement.

`startup-pixabay-mix.wav` is an edited soundtrack for the Overseer startup film:
trimmed excerpts, crossfades, level matching, panning aligned with the car, and
headroom limiting. The source recordings retain their original pitch. No
synthetic engine, tire oscillator, music, or generated audio is used in this cut.
Build: `scripts/build-startup-audio.py`.
